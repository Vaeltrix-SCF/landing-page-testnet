# Payment network hero assets

The approved abstract payment network replaces the architectural gateways in the hero only. Assets were generated with the built-in image-generation tool and converted to WebP for local delivery.

- Filled: `public/images/payment-network.webp`
- Pencil: `public/images/payment-network-pencil.webp`
- Opening component: `src/components/payment-hero.tsx`

Filled concept prompt: an original isometric monochrome payment instrument on ivory paper, with a sender stack at left, mechanical settlement ledger at center, receiving stack at right, an upper locked performance reserve, fine drafting lines and sparse orange payment signals. No bank facade, credit card, architectural gateways, text, or logos.

Pencil edit prompt: preserve the exact filled scene's composition, object locations, scale, camera, margins and proportions; remove shading, textures and orange; retain thin graphite contours on ivory paper, aligned as an overlay over the filled scene.

Animation: progressively trace the major contours, finish with the matching pencil raster, then move two clipped paper panels upward and downward from the image center. The header and hero text enter afterward. The intro can be skipped or replayed, Escape skips, and reduced-motion users receive the finished scene directly. Images load before drawing starts; a bounded fallback releases the page if loading stalls. All later page sections remain intact.
