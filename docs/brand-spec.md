# Vaeltrix landing-page direction

An original Vaeltrix interpretation of the Heron AI reference inspected in this chat. Local Next.js implementation in the existing workspace.

Audience: Stellar payment providers, wallets, and integrators. Narrative: promises become measurable commitments; covered delays become claims. Warm, authoritative drafting-board presentation at laptop and mobile viewing distances. No live deployment, adoption, or operational metrics implied.

Design read: greenfield landing page; visual variation 6/10, motion 7/10, density 5/10, illustration dependence 8/10, reference fidelity 8/10. Large illustration first; continuous sharp ruled grid; one pinned narrative; calmer supporting sections.

Palette: paper #f2f1e9, ink #272923, signal #de482b, line #c5c5b9. Typography: locally hosted Space Grotesk and IBM Plex Mono. Spacing: 8px base, 24/32px cell padding, fluid display sizing. Sharp corners, no elevated card shadows. Reveal motion 600ms with cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1); reduced motion keeps content visible and makes the story static.

Identity: Vaeltrix typographic wordmark from the project name, pending final supplied brand assets. No claim of an existing official logo. Hero: original generated architectural settlement-network illustration, saved in public/images. Other diagrams: original semantic protocol diagrams. Product illustrations use explicitly labeled examples and proposed testnet parameters, not live metrics or fabricated customers.

Interactions: menu, chapter navigation, scroll narrative, three evidence markers, scenario controls, templates, audience accordion, docs links, keyboard-accessible actions. No wallet transaction or sign-up backend.

## Vaeltrix identity revision

Palette selected for Vaeltrix: cool porcelain #edf2f6, deep navy #142b40, cobalt #245cda, slate #5c7182, and ruled lines #bccbd6. Cobalt replaces the reference's vermilion throughout. Existing hero imagery is styled with a hue rotation so the original payment signals read blue while the geometry stays intact.

The opening now reveals the finish with stationary pencil artwork: clipping boundaries expand outward from the center instead of translating the drawing halves.

## Blueprint direction

Vaeltrix now uses a deep blueprint field #082d4a, pale line/text #deedf4, cyan signal #93e4ef, structural rules #365e78, and secondary text #a6c0d1. Illustration styling projects the original graphite drawing as light blueprint linework on blue.

Decorative geometric illustrations, collateral rings, repeated process symbols, large ornamental footer wordmark, corner crosses, and closing emblem chain were removed. The collateral sheet carries real protocol constraints. Problems use compact text cells; fairness principles use a horizontal evidence-led list. Hero and payment journey diagrams remain because they explain the product.
